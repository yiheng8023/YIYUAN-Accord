# Human-AI Collaboration Learning Noru Independent Review Readiness

Date: 2026-08-01

Status: `prepared-not-reviewed-live-trial-blocked`

## Purpose

This checkpoint prepares one reproducible surface for a later independent
review of the Noru learning material. Preparation is not review. The current
task produced the packet and previously produced or calibrated part of the
material, so rereading it in the same task, invoking a review Skill, or passing
the packet validator cannot satisfy the independence gate.

The packet freezes ten files from exact commit
`3f165a447fcf10889e62e21d0821fd4ca1dc7710`: the learning route baseline,
fixed-fixture protocol, Noru public packet, evaluator-only private oracle,
material calibration, validator, negative tests, and their documents. Each
file is bound by Git Blob identity and SHA-256; the whole ordered manifest and
packet have canonical SHA-256 digests. Rebuilding requires no network, model,
account, candidate execution, participant, or external write.

## Review surface

A later distinct accountable reviewer must cover all ten axes exactly once:

- content and answer-key correctness;
- immediate/delayed form balance without claiming empirical equivalence;
- misconception coverage and falsifiers;
- readability and instruction clarity;
- accessibility and accommodation boundaries;
- public/private oracle isolation;
- cleanup, data, and participant-safety boundaries;
- claim, authority, and live-readiness separation;
- deterministic reproduction and negative controls; and
- capability reuse and the residual-gap boundary.

The public packet remains learner/tutor visible. The assessment answer key,
scoring, misconception labels, and executable private oracle remain evaluator
only and must never enter a learner or tutor workspace. A review that needs
those private files must preserve this role separation in its own handling and
receipt.

## Independence and receipt

The reviewer identity and execution process must differ from the packet
producer and previous material/calibration evaluator identities. Prior
involvement and identity evidence must be disclosed; private reasoning transfer
and target-artifact mutation are forbidden. Identity evidence remains
declarative unless a later accountable authority supplies a stronger receipt.

The receipt must preserve every axis result, finding, disagreement,
correction, limitation, and high/critical disposition. It may conclude
`accept-bounded`, `accept-with-corrections`, `reject`, or
`insufficient-evidence`. No overall outcome may be stronger than its weakest
axis. `accept-with-corrections` requires at least one structured correction
bound to a review axis and frozen manifest path, and a high or critical finding
cannot be hidden by `accept-bounded`. No outcome may exercise acceptance authority, promote
a hard standard, prove Skill necessity, authorize a live trial, or claim broad
population validity. A receipt validator proves contract shape and explicit
separation, not that the reviewer's declarations are true.

## What remains open

No independent review has been performed and no receipt exists. Content and
answer-key correctness beyond deterministic rule execution, human readability,
accessibility in use, form difficulty equivalence, misconception sufficiency,
participant safeguards, learning effect, candidate value, and live-trial
readiness remain unproved.

The packet adds no Skill, Hook, MCP, Plugin, App, account connection, CC Switch
change, global configuration change, model request, candidate execution, or
participant contact. Both linked program acceptances remain `partial`.

The next gate is a separately identified and authorized reviewer transaction.
That transaction must bind the exact packet digest, reviewer identity and
process, access to public versus private material, review scope, output
receipt, data handling, cost, stop conditions, and cleanup. Even a valid review
receipt cannot authorize a pilot; participant safeguards and live-trial
authority remain later independent decisions.
