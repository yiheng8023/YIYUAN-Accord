# Human-AI Collaboration Learning Fixed-Fixture Protocol

Date: 2026-07-31

Status: zero-model mechanism protocol validated; no live human, model,
candidate, or official-account trial

## Why this is a protocol, not a standard

The capability baseline found four representative route classes and an open
behavioral-evidence gap. This package defines how that gap could be tested. It
does not prescribe a universal learning workflow, rank a candidate, admit a
Skill, or create a hard standard.

`GEN-LEARNING-01` remains `planned-no-live-domain-evidence`. Immediate task
completion is not learning evidence. A complete-looking trial record is not an
effect. Static official documentation and Matt `teach` projection remain
capability evidence only.

## Ordered phases

1. Zero-model mechanism simulation exercises identity, authority, route,
   oracle, exposure, measurement, cleanup, and claim gates using synthetic
   records.
2. No-model material and oracle calibration creates a novel synthetic
   micro-domain, public learning packet, private oracle, misconception map,
   parallel assessments, transfer task, accessibility checklist, and cleanup
   manifest.
3. A separately authorized mechanism pilot may validate delivery, capture,
   timing, isolation, privacy, and cleanup without claiming efficacy.
4. Only then may a separately authorized and preregistered human-learning
   trial be considered.

The current work stops after phase 1. Phase 2 is the next repository-only gate.

## Comparison structure

The protocol retains four arms:

- native Codex with no configurable user Skill;
- exact Matt `teach` selected explicitly as the sole configurable user Skill;
- exactly one named official learning product on its real host and account
  boundary;
- a human instructor or mentor using the same public packet without AI.

The two Codex arms require the visible route
`gpt-5.3-codex-spark` with `low` reasoning. Silent substitution invalidates the
pair. The Matt arm additionally requires exact source identity, a
dependency-complete projection, host exposure, instruction-delivery evidence,
an isolated workspace, and an exact cleanup receipt. Installation or a
symbolic link is insufficient.

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google modes may not be merged into one fictional
official arm. A future protocol execution must select exactly one. The result
would apply to that product bundle; it could not isolate the mode from its
model and host or support a cross-provider claim.

## Fixed information and contamination boundary

Every arm receives the same complete public packet, worked examples, practice
pool, time budget, and allowed accommodation interface. No arm may receive the
private assessment oracle, hidden prior conversation, unbound external source,
or treatment-specific answer leakage.

The formal design is randomized and between-subject, stratified by pretest.
The same learner must not receive multiple treatments on the same micro-domain,
because learning in the first arm contaminates later arms. Assessment items
must not be reused as practice.

No sample size is guessed now. A formal trial needs a preregistered estimand,
pilot variance or defensible prior evidence, attrition allowance, and power
analysis. A self-test or tiny pilot can calibrate mechanisms but cannot support
a population claim.

## Non-cancelling measurements

The primary outcome is delayed unaided competence at seven days plus or minus
one day. The protocol also records pretest competence, immediate independent
competence, novel transfer, misconception correction, learner autonomy,
accessibility, teacher review, and full lifecycle cost.

Immediate correctness cannot cancel worse delayed competence, transfer,
autonomy, accessibility, adverse effects, authority violations, or incomplete
cleanup. Null results do not prove equivalence. A formally complete record is
only eligible for independent analysis.

## Zero-model simulation result

Ten fixtures currently pass. They include one valid mechanism-only record and
negative controls for:

- immediate completion promoted to learning;
- three official products merged as one arm;
- private-oracle exposure;
- missing live authority;
- silent weak-model substitution;
- Matt installation mistaken for instruction delivery;
- incomplete treatment-workspace cleanup;
- missing delayed assessment;
- a formally shaped record incorrectly upgraded beyond analysis eligibility.

The simulator dispatched no model, executed no candidate, accessed no official
account, and involved no participant. Its result is mechanism calibration only.

## Process and cleanup boundary

All treatment roots must be predeclared and disposable. Matt `teach` writes
must stay inside the exact trial root. Before-and-after manifests, external
process or window receipts when applicable, and exact cleanup verification are
mandatory. Uncertain ownership or cleanup invalidates the run. Process
artifacts become durable evidence only after review.

## Live authority still missing

This protocol does not authorize model dispatch, candidate execution, official
account access, participant recruitment or contact, a human-participant trial,
CC Switch or runtime mutation, external write or publication, Skill or Hook
authoring, hard-standard promotion, matrix evidence promotion, commit, or push.

The next action is offline material and oracle calibration. No live authority
is needed or inferred for that step.
