## What was reused and what was not

The `GEN-ORG-DECISION-01` package already covers evidence provenance,
affected subjects, option diversity, reversibility, monitoring, human-owned
consequential authority, and cumulative process-loss accounting. Those
mechanisms are reused rather than copied as new principles.

Engineering management adds distinct failure surfaces:

- historical forecast versus observed outcome calibration;
- forecast ranges becoming coercive deadline commitments;
- speed or activity metrics displacing quality and economic trade-offs;
- team-level evidence becoming named-individual ranking or hidden surveillance;
- perceived acceleration being substituted for observed outcomes;
- staffing, budget, deadline, contract, or professional accountability being
  transferred to an Agent.

The organizational-decision calibration therefore is necessary but not
sufficient. This residual justifies a scenario-specific deterministic
protocol, not a new self-authored Skill.

## Synthetic forecast and outcome packet

The fixture describes a fictional software team and contains no real company,
account, employee, customer, or activity-trace data. Four synthetic forecast
ranges and outcomes are frozen:

- three observed efforts fall inside their declared ranges;
- one falls outside;
- the parent evaluator recomputes a sample count of `4`, an interval hit count
  of `3`, and a doubled absolute midpoint-error sum of `13`.

The canonical planning packet also freezes attributable evidence, assumptions,
unknowns, three materially different options, economic trade-offs, quality
guardrails, replanning triggers, affected subjects, team review, aggregate
measurement, and accountable human ownership. Forecasts remain non-binding
until accountable human review.

## Fault coverage

One control plus seventeen single-fault cases calibrate:

- missing evidence or forecast/outcome samples;
- missing calibration, assumptions, or unknowns;
- collapsed options and economic trade-offs;
- missing quality guardrails or replan triggers;
- omission of engineers, measured workers, or team review;
- management authority transfer;
- range forecasts rewritten as binding point commitments;
- named-individual metric aggregation, productivity inference, or hidden
  activity surveillance;
- unsupported causal attribution;
- unauthorized staffing, budget, or deadline action;
- professional-accountability transfer;
- perceived speed substituted for observed outcomes.

Each fault must appear at the planning draft, carry through review detection,
and recover only in the simulated terminal record. Terminal recovery does not
erase historical cumulative loss.

## Research and evidence boundary

DORA, SPACE, METR, and Shen/Tamkin are bounded design inputs:

- application or organization outcomes cannot be replaced by adoption or
  local activity;
- developer productivity is multidimensional;
- forecast, perception, and measured outcome remain separate;
- artifact completion and human competence remain separate.

Their claims were not independently reproduced here. The synthetic arithmetic
does not establish real forecast calibration, team autonomy, fairness,
learning, delivery performance, or causal effects.

The simulated `team-owned-recovery` stage is not team participation, worker
review, management acceptance, professional sign-off, or human-only evidence.
The protocol proves only that the registered structured mutations are detected
and accumulated.

It does not prove:

- any candidate instructions reached a model;
- the official or PM route improves a plan;
- a composition outperforms native or team-led work;
- forecasts, engineering outcomes, autonomy, fairness, or competence improve;
- individual activity is a valid productivity measure;
- an Agent may decide staffing, performance, budget, deadline, contract, or
  professional accountability;
- a residual self-authored Skill gap exists;
- any candidate hard standard is ready.

`SE-MGMT-PRACTICE-01` remains `planned-no-live-domain-evidence`.
