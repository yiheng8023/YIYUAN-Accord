## Exact source-authority migration

Three identity references remain:

1. The authoritative
   `C:\Projects\codex-user-config\skills\capability-router\SKILL.md` should
   replace the hard-coded workflow stage `diagnose` with the neutral
   `diagnosis capability`. This keeps the router from acquiring a permanent
   dependency on one third-party package name.
2. The divergent CC `capability-router` copy must not be patched in place. It
   is not source authority. It must leave the execution path through the
   collision-safe first-party carrier transaction selected by the user.
3. The governed
   `skills/observability-and-instrumentation/SKILL.md` should replace
   “canonical `diagnose` Skill” with “active task-bound diagnosis workflow,”
   then be reviewed and projected through a separately authorized CC update or
   source-rebinding transaction.

The observability repair should also remove `${userId}` from its logging
example. The live CC body and governed Harness source are currently byte-equal,
so the change belongs in the governed source first; editing only the live
projection would create another authority split.

These are proposed exact source changes, not applied changes.

## Admission gates

`diagnose` remains blocked until all five gates hold:

1. select and execute the first-party carrier policy without deleting the
   current `.agents` or `.codex` source-owned trees;
2. patch, verify, and project the authoritative current `capability-router`;
3. patch, verify, and project the governed observability adaptation, including
   the PII example repair;
4. prove zero exact retained-live-body references and prove that
   `diagnosing-bugs` remains exposed on the supported target hosts;
5. rebuild manager, backup, rollback, projection, common-link, count, and
   sentinel expectations against live CC Switch 3.19.0.

Only then does the cohort grow from 14 to a conditional 15, from 19 to 21
files, and from 58,356 to 66,684 bytes.
The arithmetic `55 - 15 = 40` is not a forecast because the required
first-party carrier transaction may change the live database baseline first.
