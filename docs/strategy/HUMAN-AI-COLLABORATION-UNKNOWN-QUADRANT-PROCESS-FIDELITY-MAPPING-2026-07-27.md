# Human-AI Collaboration Unknown-Quadrant Process-Fidelity Mapping

Date: 2026-07-27
Status: research mapping validated; no method promotion; no live execution

## Result

The four classes—Known Knowns, Known Unknowns, Unknown Knowns, and
Unknown Unknowns—add a useful discovery and task-framing lens to the current
human-AI collaboration program. They do not replace the existing architecture
and do not authorize a new Skill.

The primary method source is Anthropic's 2026-07-06 article,
`A field guide to Claude Fable 5: Finding your unknowns`. The user also supplied
a more detailed Chinese Bilibili breakdown. Both are dated external research
inputs. Their current web content is not byte-bound and must be rechecked before
material reuse.

## Layer boundary

- **Hard standards are controls, not Skill treatments.** They remain mandatory
  and identical across treatment arms; no candidate receives credit merely for
  obeying them.
- The unknown-quadrant mapping is a research and framing lens, not a hard
  standard or globally mandatory workflow.
- `intent-contract`, `capability-router`, and `closure-contract` remain
  self-authored phase controls, not one domain-method treatment.
- Matt and Superpowers remain selective, task-matched external method
  candidates. Source identity does not prove behavior, value, or superiority.
- Runner, ledger, and process-fidelity accounting remain infrastructure.
- CC Switch remains the applicable source, install, update, and projection
  manager; it is not a method-value oracle.

## Quadrant mapping

### Known Knowns

Bind explicit facts, scope, authority, repository truth, evidence, and hard
standards. Preserve them through handoff and transformation. A single
unaccounted explicit-fact loss falsifies process-fidelity acceptance even when
the terminal answer is plausible.

### Known Unknowns

Record explicit uncertainties and their decision impact. Ask the smallest
high-impact blocking question, run only bounded authorized checks, and keep
assumptions distinct from facts. Matt's grilling methods or Superpowers
brainstorming may be treatment candidates for a bound task; invoking one does
not prove that an uncertainty was resolved correctly.

### Unknown Knowns

Elicit tacit taste and know-it-when-seen criteria through small prototypes,
variants, references, and concrete contrasts. Consequential preferences remain
human decisions. Silence, one prototype, or Agent preference must not become
confirmed user preference.

### Unknown Unknowns

Run a proportional blind-spot pass across relevant roles, code paths, lifecycle
edges, official sources, and authorized external evidence. Discovery turns an
issue into a known or known unknown; discovery is not authority, and a scan
cannot prove that no hidden issue exists.

## Lifecycle use

Before implementation, use a blind-spot pass, targeted questions, references,
small prototypes, and a decision-first plan where risk justifies them.

During implementation, record material deviations with the reason, affected
invariant, authority impact, and recovery anchor. This is proportional evidence,
not a mandatory universal diary.

After implementation, explanations and optional quizzes or teach-back may
improve user understanding. They cannot substitute for tests, independent
acceptance, authorization, closeout evidence, or cleanup accounting.

## Process-loss and weak-Agent boundary

The mapping refines fixtures and attribution oracles for the existing chained
transform and TDD work. It does not dispatch Spark, Luna, Matt, Superpowers, or
the self-authored chain. Deterministic zero-model fixtures remain first. Any
weak-Agent run requires the exact current-task grant, fresh host-reported route,
runner preflight, and ledger reservation already required by the successor
contract.

Terminal correctness and intermediate process fidelity remain separate. A
correct final result cannot erase a lost explicit fact, an unlabelled guess, an
invented preference, or a skipped predeclared perspective.

## Decision

Use this mapping to refine scenario fixtures and method-attribution oracles.
Compare native behavior, Matt, Superpowers, and the existing phase controls
only on named capability gaps. Self-authoring remains behind repeatable
residual-gap evidence and separate authority.

This record does not authorize installation, CC Switch mutation, model
dispatch, Skill invocation, portfolio mutation, global configuration change,
or a production-readiness claim.
