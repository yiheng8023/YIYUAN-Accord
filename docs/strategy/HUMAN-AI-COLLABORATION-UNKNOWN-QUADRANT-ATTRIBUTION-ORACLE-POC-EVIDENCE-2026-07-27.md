Date: 2026-07-27

## Result

A new additive fixture and evaluator layer classifies 22 deterministic cases
across Known Knowns, Known Unknowns, Unknown Knowns, Unknown Unknowns,
method attribution, weak-route identity, and residual-gap admission. All 22
matched their predeclared expected classification.

The 2026-07-23 historical Skill-overlap fixtures were not rewritten. This PoC
extends them with a dated overlay so previous evidence remains interpretable.

## Four unknown-class oracles

- **Known Knowns:** every explicit fact must be retained or changed only through
  a separately authorized, recorded delta.
- **Known Unknowns:** every material explicit uncertainty must be resolved with
  evidence or remain visibly bounded and open.
- **Unknown Knowns:** consequential human preference must be confirmed by the
  human or remain unresolved.
- **Unknown Unknowns:** every predeclared relevant perspective must be inspected,
  but the scan can never prove completeness or absence of hidden issues.

## Method-attribution firewall

Hard standards remain shared controls and cannot be credited to a Skill.
Matt and Superpowers remain separately isolated external method candidates.
The self-authored `intent-contract`, `capability-router`, and
`closure-contract` chain remains phase control rather than a domain-method
treatment. Runner and ledger behavior remains infrastructure.

Two method candidates active in the same non-isolated arm are confounded.
Payload presence or self-reported use does not prove invocation. Even an
observed invocation plus a private-oracle match is only one attributed outcome,
not net value or superiority. Terminal correctness cannot rescue failed process
fidelity.

## Weak route and residual-gap firewall

`gpt-5.3-codex-spark` at `low`, with a fresh host route receipt, remains the
primary weak-Agent condition. Luna/low remains capacity diagnostic only after a
named weak failure. Route identity alone does not prove method value.

Residual-gap review requires eligible external candidates to have been
evaluated and at least three independent weak-Agent failures attributable to
the same method gap. Hard-standard, runner, ledger, transport, or host failures
do not count as method-gap evidence. Crossing that threshold opens design
review; it does not authorize self-authoring.
