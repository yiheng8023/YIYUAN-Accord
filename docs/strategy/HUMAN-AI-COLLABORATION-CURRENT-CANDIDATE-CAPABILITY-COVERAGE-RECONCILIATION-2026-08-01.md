## Purpose

This checkpoint consolidates the candidate work that accumulated after the
2026-07-27 scenario coverage audit. It maps current native (`N`), official
(`O`), reviewed external (`E`), composed (`C`), human-control (`H`), and
residual/self-authored (`R`) routes across all thirteen governed scenarios.

It is a subtraction and sequencing record, not a new candidate catalog. It
uses nine exact repository sources and performs no web discovery, installation,
candidate execution, model request, CC Switch mutation, or global
configuration mutation.

## Evidence vocabulary

The map keeps distinct ceilings for contract-only, source-static, conditional,
no-model, bounded synthetic association, and mechanism-calibration evidence.
None means instruction delivery, candidate causation, live-domain value,
cross-host value, executed human control, or residual self-authored need.

`unassessed` is not a failure and is not an installation backlog. A route may
be reconsidered only after a bound task exposes a specific mechanism gap,
source or host health changes, or a separate-arm comparison leaves an
attributable composition gap.

## Overlap and conflict

Six overlap groups keep commonly conflated responsibilities separate:

- creative decision elicitation versus production;
- host learning guidance versus a durable external workspace and educator
  control;
- official evidence/KPI analysis versus external affected-party/red-team
  structure and organizational authority;
- message semantics versus document carrier, translation service,
  accessibility, recipient evidence, and release;
- delivery pipeline design versus final release readiness; and
- ordinary native reasoning versus external software methods without loader
  causation.

Five conflict groups fail closed on human-authority substitution, composition
attribution, account/data/network/write expansion, host/source/version drift,
and lifecycle/cleanup cost. Every scenario fallback begins with the native
route and ends at an accountable human control. Residual
self-authoring never appears in a fallback.

## Dimension and lifecycle projection

The same route cells are projected onto all nine provisional evaluation axes
and all fourteen software-lifecycle slices already named by the scenario
matrix. The projection is a coverage coordinate, not a score. Every axis and
lifecycle row retains `liveDomainValueProved=false`.

This means the provisional ontology is broad enough to index the current
candidate evidence, but the evidence does not yet validate the ontology as a
standard. Candidate comparison may still subtract, merge, or revise its
dimensions later.

## Decision

Representative coverage is mapped well enough to stop untargeted
candidate-name discovery. The fifteen unassessed cells do not justify bulk
installation, and static overlap does not justify retirement. Composition
waits for separate-arm attribution. Missing behavioral evidence does not
justify a new Skill, Hook, MCP controller, or hard standard.

The next bounded comparison must select one decision-relevant behavioral or
mechanism gap and bind its task, current source and host identity, authority,
oracle, fallback, cleanup, and claim ceiling. The map itself authorizes no live
comparison.
