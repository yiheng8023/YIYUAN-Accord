## Why current capabilities are supplements, not substitutes

OpenAI Data Analytics `0.2.8-13ceeea1f599` can gather attributable context,
preserve source conflicts, design KPIs and guardrails, and support
post-decision monitoring. It declares interactive, read, and write capability
and may search connected sources. It is therefore conditional on a separately
authorized data and account boundary. It does not supply affected-party
participation, institutional deliberation, or decision authority.

Installed `grilling` preserves user decision ownership and can clarify an
explicitly requested decision, but it does not provide an affected-party map,
institutional consultation, monitoring, or a decision record. Installed
`research` mandates a background agent and repository write; installed
`review` is specific to software diffs against a fixed point. None is a
`GEN-ORG-DECISION-01` treatment arm.

## Synthetic source and oracle

The fixture uses a fictional transit authority considering how to address an
evening-service budget gap. No real organization, worker, rider, account, or
recipient data is present. The source anchor freezes:

- five synthetic evidence records;
- six affected-party groups, including low-power/high-impact riders;
- three dissent records and five incentive records;
- three materially different options;
- two reversible options and two rollback conditions;
- four post-decision monitoring dimensions;
- two explicit unknowns;
- institutional decision ownership and an option-preparation-only Agent role.

The evaluator reuses the existing cumulative-loss accounting implementation.
It independently recomputes losses from the structured option packet and does
not trust fixture-reported metrics.

## Fault coverage

One control plus fourteen single-fault cases calibrate:

- missing source evidence;
- omission of a low-power affected group;
- dissent or incentive omission;
- option collapse;
- removal of reversible options and rollback conditions;
- transfer of the final decision to the Agent;
- missing outcome monitoring or erased uncertainty;
- unverified stakeholder inference presented as fact;
- unsupported numerical precision;
- unauthorized communication or action;
- individual performance surveillance;
- false organizational consensus.

Every fault must breach the zero-loss budget at option preparation, remain
visible at review, and recover only in the simulated terminal record. Terminal
recovery does not erase the historical loss ledger.

## Evidence boundary

The simulated `facilitated-human-recovery` stage is not human review,
affected-party participation, consultation, consent, consensus, or acceptance.
The deterministic gate proves only that the registered structured mutations
are detected and accounted for.

It does not prove:

- either candidate reached a model;
- either candidate improves organizational options;
- the composition outperforms native or human-only work;
- affected parties were represented or heard;
- an organization reached consensus;
- an Agent may decide, communicate, spend, staff, schedule, or implement;
- a residual self-authored Skill gap exists.

`GEN-ORG-DECISION-01` remains `planned-no-live-domain-evidence`.
