# AI-Era Classical Software-Engineering Principles Revalidation

## Status and purpose

This is a bounded research and evaluation contract. It does not restore a
pre-AI process model, promote a hard standard, create a Skill or Hook, justify
self-authored capability work, or change the current execution order.

The supported bounded finding is:

> AI can reduce the cost of producing code while leaving intent formation,
> correctness judgment, responsibility, integration, verification, and
> long-term maintenance costly. Classical engineering principles therefore
> require AI-era revalidation rather than blanket revival or dismissal.

The economic value of the Harness is not the only value surface. Intent
fidelity, epistemic integrity, human judgment and authorization, accountable
evidence, reversibility, continuity, resilience, and long-term system health
are also first-class evaluation dimensions.

## Primary-source synthesis

The current evidence is mixed rather than uniformly pro- or anti-AI:

- [DORA 2025](https://dora.dev/research/2025/dora-report/) describes AI as an
  amplifier of the underlying organizational and delivery system.
- [DORA, Balancing AI tensions](https://dora.dev/insights/balancing-ai-tensions/)
  reports that faster generation shifts effort into auditing and verification
  and can combine higher throughput with higher instability.
- [Microsoft Research field experiments](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-effects-of-generative-ai-on-high-skilled-work-evidence-from-three-field-experiments-with-software-developers/)
  found higher completed-task throughput across a large developer cohort, with
  heterogeneous effects.
- [METR's early-2025 randomized study](https://metr.org/Early_2025_AI_Experienced_OS_Devs_Study-paper.pdf)
  found experienced maintainers slower on familiar mature repositories despite
  expecting a speedup.
- [GitHub's randomized code-quality study](https://github.blog/news-insights/research/does-github-copilot-improve-code-quality-heres-what-the-data-says/)
  found bounded quality improvements on one narrow Python task; it is a
  vendor-run, task-specific result rather than a lifecycle-wide conclusion.
- [Microsoft Research on bounded delegation](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/to-copilot-and-beyond-22-ai-systems-developers-want-built/)
  records developer demand for earlier quality signals, explicit authority,
  provenance, and uncertainty as generation accelerates.
- [USENIX Security 2025](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity25/presentation/spracklen)
  found persistent package hallucination risk, reinforcing dependency
  provenance and supply-chain verification.
- [GitHub Spec Kit](https://github.github.com/spec-kit/index.html) and
  [AWS Kiro](https://aws.amazon.com/documentation-overview/kiro/) demonstrate
  an industry move toward iterative, Agent-consumable specifications and
  quality gates. Product direction is not independent effectiveness proof.

These sources do not establish one universal AI productivity or quality
effect. They support revalidation against task type, developer experience,
repository maturity, organizational delivery system, model and tool version,
verification surface, and total lifecycle cost.

## Classification vocabulary

Every candidate is classified as exactly one of:

1. **candidate-invariant** — the underlying obligation remains meaningful
   across AI and non-AI execution, subject to later repeated evidence and
   separate admission;
2. **adaptive-practice** — the goal remains useful but its implementation
   should vary with task, host, risk, reversibility, and lifecycle phase;
3. **obsolete-ceremony** — the historical process form lacks supported net
   value or is better replaced by a smaller deterministic or adaptive path;
4. **insufficient-evidence** — current evidence cannot support promotion,
   rejection, or mandatory use.

Classification is not admission. A candidate invariant is not a hard standard,
and an obsolete ceremony label requires evidence rather than preference.

## Required evaluation dimensions

Each future candidate package must bind:

- the original failure mode it was intended to prevent;
- the AI-era effect: amplified, reduced, transformed, unchanged, or unknown;
- current native, official, reviewed-external, and composed coverage;
- deterministic verification that does not depend on an Agent claiming success;
- the residual human or domain-judgment boundary;
- creativity, context, latency, operational, and maintenance cost;
- scope, counterexamples, falsifiers, and safe fallback;
- a revalidation or retirement condition.

## Relationship to existing acceptance

This lens extends, but does not add to, the current acceptance inventory:

- `acceptance.software-engineering-lifecycle-specialization`;
- `acceptance.ai-independent-hard-standard-boundary`;
- `acceptance.standard-candidate-contract`;
- `acceptance.adaptive-harness-proportionality`.

The existing `standard-candidate-contract` remains partial. This research
contract supplies a checked-in schema and bounded fixture, but not repeated
cross-host value, CALIBRATION review, ASSETS admission, or hard-standard
authority.

## Goal-mode resume delta

When the paused goal is resumed, append this decision constraint:

> Treat AI-era revalidation of classical software-engineering principles as a
> horizontal research and evaluation lens. Separate candidate invariants,
> adaptive practices, obsolete ceremonies, and insufficient-evidence items;
> assess original failure mode, AI-era effect, current ecosystem coverage,
> deterministic verification, residual human judgment, creativity and
> maintenance cost, counterexamples, and retirement conditions. Do not restore
> rigid legacy process because a principle regained importance, and do not
> weaken intent, evidence, responsibility, verification, rollback, or long-term
> system-health requirements because AI improved generation speed. This lens
> does not reorder current candidate work, prove a self-authored gap, or promote
> a hard standard without separate repeated evidence and authority.

## Explicit non-authorizations

This checkpoint authorizes no external capability discovery or installation,
CC Switch change, Skill or Hook mutation, model dispatch, cross-repository
write, CALIBRATION or ASSETS admission, hard-standard promotion, commit, push,
or release.
