## Outcome

The three self-authored Skills are not accepted as the correct final
architecture. They remain provisional experimental carriers only.

- `intent-contract` materially overlaps the global source-binding, intake, and
  authority gates.
- `capability-router` materially overlaps the global reuse order, authorization
  boundary, and reroute rules.
- `closure-contract` materially overlaps the global closure and status-mutation
  gates.

Those overlaps require hard-only comparison. They do not by themselves prove
that the Skills should be deleted: a reusable cross-project role remains
plausible, but its incremental value is unproved.

The common `~/.agents/skills` root is retained independently of the disposition
of any current body. It is a compatibility surface for present or future
Agents, not a requirement that Codex and Claude carry byte-identical effective
packages. Host-specific differences are acceptable when their portable
semantics, source lineage, authority, and verification boundary are explicit.

## Native, official, external, and composition boundary

Codex app-server supplies official mechanisms for Skill inventory and explicit
input, Hook discovery and execution, thread lifecycle, manual compaction, Apps,
Plugins, and related runtime surfaces. These mechanisms carry or observe
behavior; they do not independently perform the semantic intake, cross-
ecosystem routing, or cross-task closeout judgments claimed by the three
Skills.

Reviewed Matt and Superpowers workflows cover narrower subflows:

- Matt grilling, ask-matt, code-review, and handoff;
- Superpowers brainstorming, using-superpowers, and
  verification-before-completion.

They should be reused before another generic or domain Skill is authored, but
current metadata and historical mixed live cells do not prove that either
suite replaces the complete control chain. No full-suite bootstrap is promoted
to a global default.

## Lifecycle and continuity

The chain covers three control checkpoints—intake, route selection, and
closeout sufficiency. It is not a complete software lifecycle and does not
replace requirements, architecture, implementation, security, release,
operations, or maintenance workflows.

The repository continuation protocol remains useful navigation. Native thread
and compaction surfaces exist, but automatic compression quality, Skill-loader
delivery, receiver recovery, cross-host adherence, and lossless continuity
remain unproved.

## Subtractive disposition

No new control Skill is justified. No Skill, Hook, CC Switch entry, duplicate
projection, or reviewed external suite is installed, promoted, replaced,
retired, or deleted by this audit. The allowed final admitted self-authored
payload count remains zero.

The exact current three-Skill chain is retained only to preserve the frozen
factorial experiment. Under the current exact-loader acceptance boundary, the
host-observability gap prevents live value attribution. Static overlap cannot
be upgraded into a portfolio decision.

The current Codex turn additionally exhibits behavior consistent with implicit
Skill selection. It does not identify whether native/runtime discovery, global
rules, the self-authored Skill bodies, Hook recall, or their composition caused
that behavior. This observation requires a native-first causal ablation before
any update, reduction, or retirement decision; it is not evidence that the
native host supersedes the chain or that the chain has incremental value.

The separate 2026-07-29 same-prompt sentinel ablation now completes that
native-capability check for Codex `0.145.0`: without the sentinel, the control
did not emit the private body token; with the sentinel, Codex visibly read the
full `SKILL.md` and returned the exact private token. A temporary
`CODEX_HOME`, the live empty Hook surface, temporary removal of the three
self-authored `.agents` Skills, and an out-of-repository work root excluded the
known custom carriers. The common `.agents/skills` directory remained present,
and all live payloads were restored.

Native implicit Skill invocation is therefore a verified current-host baseline.
It does not prove that an earlier mixed-carrier turn was native-caused or that
the three self-authored Skills are redundant. Their next review must compare
distinct semantics, trigger quality, portability, context cost, failure
fallback, and incremental value over this baseline.
