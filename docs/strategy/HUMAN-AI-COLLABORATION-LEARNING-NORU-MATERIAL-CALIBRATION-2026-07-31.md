# Human-AI Collaboration Learning Material Calibration

Date: 2026-07-31

Status: offline structural and oracle-logic calibration passed; independent
human review remains open

## Material

The first fixed material is the synthetic `Noru cards` micro-domain. A card is
described entirely by three text fields: band, mark, and count. Five public
rules define two operations, left-to-right composition, and compatibility with
an exception. No color, image, audio, personal history, educational record, or
employment context is needed.

The public packet contains the full rule reference, plain-language glossary,
four worked examples, eight practice items, interaction limits, accessibility
options, and a minimum-data boundary. It contains no assessment answer,
scoring point, misconception label, or private-oracle identifier.

The private oracle contains a three-item general rule-application pretest, six
immediate items, six delayed items, three novel-transfer items, eight
misconception definitions, scoring boundaries, and the known form difference.
It is evaluator-only and must never enter a learner or tutor workspace.

## Deterministic result

The validator executes the Noru transformation and compatibility rules rather
than trusting answer strings. All twelve structured immediate and delayed
cases and the inverse transfer case are consistent. All misconception
references resolve. Practice and assessment IDs are disjoint. Immediate and
delayed forms both contain six items and fourteen points. Public assessment
answer leakage is zero.

This is not empirical form equivalence. The immediate form directly tests
plain echo wrap, while the delayed form directly tests plain echo advance;
both exercise the complementary behavior inside composition. That visible
difference must be challenged during review and pilot calibration instead of
being hidden behind an equivalence label.

## Accessibility and data boundary

All task meaning is carried by text labels. The packet supports linear
screen-reader order, larger text, restatement without new information, extra
response time recorded as an accommodation, and typed or transcribed spoken
responses without scoring delivery style. An accessibility barrier is not a
domain misconception.

Only a pseudonymous trial identifier belongs in a future trial record. The
materials request no identity, education, employment, disability, or personal
history data. A later participant trial still needs a separate consent,
privacy, withdrawal, retention, and safeguarding review.

## Cleanup manifest

A future trial root must resolve under
`.tmp/aah-learning-noru/<trial-id>`. Generated public material, treatment
workspace, host receipts, pseudonymous trial record, and cleanup receipt must
remain inside that exact root. The private oracle, credentials, account
exports, raw chat history, and personal profiles are forbidden there.

Before-and-after manifests are mandatory. Any external process or window needs
a receipt. The exact root must be removed or receive an explicit governed
retention decision. Uncertain ownership fails closed.

## What remains unproved

The current producer performed the deterministic structural calibration, so
independent content review remains open. Human readability, accessibility in
use, immediate-versus-delayed form difficulty, misconception coverage,
participant safeguards, and statistical power are unproved. No model,
candidate, official account, or participant was used.

The package does not prove human learning, candidate value, live-trial
readiness, a Skill gap, or a standard. The next gate is a separately identified
review of content, balance, readability, accessibility, and cleanup boundaries;
live treatment remains blocked.
