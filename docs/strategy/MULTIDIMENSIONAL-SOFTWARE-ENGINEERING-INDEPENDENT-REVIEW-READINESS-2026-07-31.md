## Decision

Preparation is not review. This checkpoint freezes the evaluation contract,
both bounded assessments, the source observation and deterministic snapshot,
their validators and tests, and the relevant program projections at exact Git
commit `c634d83d84b269ab66a94eba1540d04133e308dd`.

The packet contains nineteen Git blob identities, SHA-256 content digests, and
a canonical manifest digest. It can be reconstructed without network access or
a model. No review receipt is generated by the producing task.

## Independence boundary

A Different execution identity and a different process or task identifier are
required. The reviewer must disclose prior involvement and provide the best
available identity evidence. The current task, either assessment evaluator, or
a same-task reread cannot satisfy the independence declaration. Calling
`review`, `code-review`, or another workflow Skill does not by itself create an
independent subject or process.

The packet producer transfers frozen artifacts and public review questions,
not private reasoning. The reviewer may not mutate the reviewed target while
forming the receipt. These declarations are structurally validated, but the
repository cannot cryptographically prove a human or Agent identity that the
host does not attest.

## Review surface

Rights, evidence, conflict, and plural-subject review are mandatory alongside
repository standards and specification fidelity. The ten non-cancelling axes
cover:

- specification and repository standards;
- source rights and bounded use;
- source identity, evidence strength, and freshness;
- conflicts, ambiguity, counterevidence, and disagreement;
- dimension, candidate-floor, profile, and next-evidence judgments;
- plural affected subjects and the anti-deference boundary;
- claim, authority, independent-review, and acceptance separation;
- Git provenance, portability, and CRLF/LF byte semantics;
- deterministic reconstruction and negative controls;
- capability reuse and residual-gap boundaries.

Each axis receives its own bounded outcome. A reviewer must keep high or
critical finding disposition, disagreement, corrections, and limitations
visible. A single overall outcome cannot cancel an axis finding.

## Receipt and authority

The future receipt may say `accept-bounded`, `accept-with-corrections`,
`reject`, or `insufficient-evidence`. It is still a review record, not an
acceptance record. It does not authorize acceptance, release, hard-standard
promotion, broad-population validity, or a new Skill.

No new evaluation Skill is justified by this preparation. The existing Matt
review Skills contribute a useful standards/specification separation, while
the repository thin-slice contributes producer/reviewer separation, exact
artifact binding, independent re-execution, oracle isolation, and risk
disposition. The prepared packet composes those ideas without executing a
Skill or asserting reviewer independence.
