candidate-specific configurable metadata selection for both treatments. It
also proves that the projection and no-turn inventory mechanism can preserve
the observed repository, global configuration, and candidate bytes on this
host.

independent loader event, does not prove that Skill instructions reached the
model, and does not establish behavior, causation, candidate superiority,
installed-plugin delivery, cross-host value, general TDD competence, or
production readiness.

The raw reports and projection manifests remain temporary. Their internal
canonical hashes and raw file hashes are retained in the registry evidence,
but no claim depends on those temporary paths remaining available. The
repository validator cross-checks the reviewed per-file summary against the
parent protocol; it does not replay or independently prove the original
app-server session. Tool hashes are observation-time identities, not a rule
that prevents later tool evolution.
