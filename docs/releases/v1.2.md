# Agent Autonomy Harness v1.2

This finite open-source release is a bounded collaboration contract and
calibration baseline. It is not a universal Agent runtime or a claim that all
human-AI collaboration problems are solved.

## Included claims

- `portable-contract-conformance`
- `thin-codex-and-claude-projection-conformance`
- `bounded-representative-help-and-interference-results`
- `clean-checkout-reproducibility`

The representative sample retains GT-01 and GT-07 as bounded passes and GT-02
as a failure. GT-02 cleanup behavior is therefore excluded from host
qualification and from this release's success claims.

The two thin packages share the K/H/L contract but retain host-required
packaging differences. They have no dependency on a user-level Skill manager
or on separately governed personal Skills being installed or enabled.

## Not implied

- `universal-human-ai-collaboration-correctness`
- `broad-field-effectiveness`
- `comparative-burden-reduction-across-populations`
- `all-agent-or-all-host-equivalence`
- `longitudinal-human-or-organizational-outcomes`
- `domain-professional-correctness`
- `production-deployment-safety`
- `every-golden-task-or-host-projection-passes`

Field effect, cross-host equivalence and longitudinal outcomes remain explicit
post-release evidence lanes. Later evidence may narrow, revise or retire this
baseline.
