# YIYUAN Accord positioning and lifecycle framework

> Status: accepted high-weight project analysis and design input.
> Date: 2026-08-21.
> Boundary: this report informs product authority but is not itself authority,
> implementation evidence, field-effect proof or release authorization.

## 1. Position and mission

YIYUAN Accord is an open, Agent-neutral portable human-Agent collaboration
reliability contract and evaluation framework. It is not a runtime, platform,
workflow engine, capability marketplace, model router, identity system,
monitoring service or domain authority.

Its long-term mission is to reduce intent loss, state drift, authority
confusion, evidence mismatch, uncontrolled effects and coordination burden in
human-AI collaboration. The current product makes only the narrower claims
supported by tool-using Agent workflows and replaceable host projections.

The project exists only while a consequential residual gap remains and its
benefit exceeds total lifecycle cost. Native or maintained ecosystem
improvement may simplify, replace or retire any project-specific mechanism.

## 2. Collaboration model

Intent, Capability, Authority and Evidence are the four first diagnostic
questions:

- What result and phase currently govern?
- Which actor is observably fit for the bounded work?
- Who may decide or create the effect?
- What supports the claim at the consequence level asserted?

They are not an exhaustive ontology. State, Effect, Continuity and Lifecycle
cross-cut all four questions. Responsibility names an assigned duty;
Accountability names ownership of consequential judgment and residual risk;
neither is interchangeable with Authority or legal liability.

The canonical terms are defined in `CONTEXT.md`. Product semantics remain in
the three JSON authority files.

## 3. Portable core and evolution principles

The portable core remains K1 through K5:

1. Goal First.
2. Minimum Sufficient Route.
3. Human Authority.
4. Continuous Reconciliation.
5. Close the Loop.

Five evolution principles govern mechanism admission without becoming five
new components:

- Reality First: a declared state, artifact or test is not the real result.
- Subtraction: revisit premises, duplication and native coverage before adding.
- Restraint: technical ability does not expand product responsibility.
- Fallback: contain observable divergence, recover where possible and stop
  honestly where support ends.
- Residual Gap Filling: add only the smallest intervention for an evidenced,
  consequential and otherwise under-owned gap.

Ecosystem complementarity and replaceability are required. The product may
offer a better portable contract or evaluation surface, but it does not create
lock-in or duplicate sufficient native behavior.

## 4. Bounded guarantees and evidence

No system can guarantee discovery of every failure. YIYUAN Accord increases
detectability, containment, recovery and claim honesty only within its observed
and technically controllable boundary. Unsupported state remains `unknown`,
and consequential action stops before authority or evidence is invented.

Trace and evidence are distinct. A prompt, report, plan, commit, test, log or
Agent statement may support a claim but never promotes its own result. Human
review counts only when the reviewer has suitable competence, evidence,
alternatives, time, accessible presentation, a real veto or escalation path
and accountable ownership.

The product does not claim universal human-AI correctness, domain-professional
correctness, production safety, all-host equivalence or longitudinal human and
organizational benefit without separate applicable evidence.

## 5. Mechanism lifecycle and evaluation

Every mechanism records:

```yaml
reason: observed residual gap
scope: actors, resources, effects and duration
alternatives: native, maintained, human, domain and no-action routes
benefit: expected help or risk reduction
cost: code, instruction, state, evidence, cognition, recovery and maintenance
verification: required observations and falsifiers
owner: accountable lifecycle owner
retirement: disable, replace or remove trigger
```

Admission follows this order:

```text
observe -> classify -> challenge premise -> compare alternatives
        -> run a bounded reversible trial when uncertain
        -> admit or reject -> verify help and interference
        -> simplify, replace or retire
```

Evaluation measures outcome quality, risk and unauthorized effects, human
burden, framework interference, recovery, residue and continuing learning.
Serious or repeated failure changes a bounded evaluation, route, instruction
or mechanism only after causal and abstraction review; anecdotes do not create
permanent layers.

## 6. Evidence-driven evolution

YIYUAN Accord does not pre-authorize expansion into multi-Agent, multi-tool,
multi-organization or high-responsibility domains. Each begins as a separate
field hypothesis with affected people, authority, data, cost and claim limits.

The continuing lanes are:

- maintain the current portable baseline;
- observe real user help, burden, interference and recovery;
- admit new failures only through the mechanism lifecycle;
- revalidate host projections when host behavior changes;
- remove logic whose residual value is no longer demonstrated.

Commercial use is a hypothesis, not product proof. Enterprise and
high-responsibility use require their own professional, legal, security,
privacy, reliability and production authorities. Project retirement is a
successful outcome if the ecosystem absorbs the remaining value; mechanism
retirement is the routine path that keeps the project small meanwhile.
