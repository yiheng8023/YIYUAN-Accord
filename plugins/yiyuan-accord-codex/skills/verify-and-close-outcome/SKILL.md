# Verify and close the outcome

Use the current goal and authority. If Accord's coordination duties are absent,
read the [brief entry](../deliver-demand-driven-outcome/SKILL.md). Keep verification
proportional to consequences; this Skill neither adds deliverables nor grants access.

## Correct changes and failures

Use source, dependency and execution evidence to trace consequential direct and
indirect effects across the accepted task, consumers and shared resources. Changes
to requirements, facts, permissions, baselines or acceptance reopen affected earlier
results and conclusions this task relies on. Include user, host and other-component
changes; ownership of Accord is not the boundary of impact. New findings grant no
rights over unrelated tasks or their historical debt.

Preserve original observations and their conditions. Qualify or withdraw invalid
conclusions, repair affected work within authority, reconcile plans and progress,
and verify the results and dependencies that must remain valid. A future plan or
change list does not complete correction. Carry consequential unknowns in native
task state or an existing checkpoint's `unresolved` list; file matches cannot close
that gap. An explicit reason is not evidence or a new user decision.

After failure, inspect actual effects and partial artifacts before selecting a
supported recovery. Distinguish missing evidence from an earlier prerequisite that
never held: later verification cannot retroactively satisfy it. Preserve the failed
sequence, reuse valid effects and bind further checks to their actual conditions.
Keep resolved failures in an account of encountered issues and distinguish current
blockers. Repeat an attempt only for a changed premise or a discriminating check.
Execute and verify the correction while independent safe work continues.

## Verify the required result

Compare consequential claims with actual sources and every affected artifact;
retain domain-specific correctness and non-compensable quality floors. Start with
existing checks or direct comparisons for one-off outputs. Missing operational
facts remain unresolved; plausible conventions cannot establish readiness.

For a maintained checker or consequential gate, exercise valid source variations,
dependent outputs and plausible false positives. An expected value can repeat the
implementation's error. For free text, include counterexamples that preserve the
expected words while contradicting a required fact, relationship or permission.
Use adequate semantic review or a verifiable representation; report remaining gaps.
A checker proves only its actual predicates, and agreement between outputs does not
replace source checking or anomaly detection.

Use [capability coordination](../coordinate-capabilities/SKILL.md) when a material
gap warrants independent review. Reuse unchanged valid evidence and batch independent
reads when supported; keep dependent writes and decisions ordered. Recheck for new
changes, failures or unresolved risks, not to repeat a pass. Necessary outcomes and
quality do not shrink to fit a budget.

A benefit claim needs an adequate native comparison and accounting for models,
configuration, memory, helpers, quality and total cost. Installation, injection,
invocation, evaluator rescue or a historical pass does not prove ordinary behavior,
current coverage or value. Keep actual package/host conditions and evidence scope.

## Release owned resources and close out

Classify ownership against starting material and current authority. Being unused
this phase does not make an original disposable; output-only limits constrain new
artifacts. Verify every protected original, including those not analyzed this phase.
Release attributable allocations, task-local bindings and temporary exposure,
check post-state, and
preserve shared or unknown ownership and needed effects. Distinguish stopping use,
disabling, uninstalling and retiring. For uncertain reuse, prefer reversible
inactivity and weigh actual reuse, expiry and upkeep before authorized retirement.
Completion grants no permission to remove user-selected tools or other components.

For installation, upgrade or removal itself, use
[plugin lifecycle](../manage-plugin-lifecycle/SKILL.md). Continue safe authorized
unfinished work or identify the actual decision, wait or execution boundary.
Report verified results, limitations and remaining duties. Functional coverage,
outcome completion, proven value and release are distinct claims; commit, push and
publication follow the bound authority.
